## Notes on the transcription itself

- The date reads *"perhaps seventh September"* — the *perhaps* is written, not
  an artefact of the hand being hard to read. It is left in.
- *"certainity"* is spelled that way on the page. Kept.
- The bracket after **Itself** groups *"the only certainity"* and *"change"* as
  one figure: change is what itself is certain of. The rendering above is the
  closest ASCII gets; read the image for the shape.
- The first arrow under **CURA** is drawn as a correction — *sees* revised to
  *observes* — rather than as two separate items.
- The faint pencil marks in the upper third are show-through from the reverse
  of the sheet, not content.

## What already sits beside it in this repository

The page did not arrive alone. Three files here carry the same thought in the
founder's own compressed form, and reading them together is the point:

| File | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `Intent` | *change is certain to me . want something from it .* |

`Pattern`'s **aforementioned** and the page's **Perception playing
Aforementioned** are the same word doing the same work: the aforementioned is
*change*, and both the pattern and the act of seeing are woven inside it rather
than applied to it from outside.

`Glimpse` is the org door stated as an appetite rather than a format —
*greeted with glimpse, serves depth to the asker.* The three-line door in
[`comfortcurators/.github`](https://github.com/comfortcurators/.github/blob/main/CLAUDE.md)
is that sentence turned into a shape.

`README.md` closes the same figure numerically: `0` branching to `1` and `2`,
both labelled `(PATTERN)` — *"two zeros, cause one became itself through two."*
